07 / Executive frameworks & intelligence

Systems thinking
for enterprise performance.

Original working models for connecting strategy, transformation, risk, intelligence, value and evolution. These are proposed frameworks, not established industry standards.

STRIVE framework

Strategy → Transformation → Risk → Intelligence → Value → Evolution.

S

Strategy

Define direction, choices, position and intent.

T

Transformation

Mobilize people, process, platforms and change.

R

Risk

Make exposure, controls and accountability visible.

I

Intelligence

Turn data and signals into better decisions.

V

Value

Connect delivery to adoption, benefits and return.

E

Evolution

Learn, adapt and strengthen the operating model.

Executive decision model

Observe → Orient → Decide → Act → Learn.

Use a repeatable loop to reduce delay, surface assumptions and make learning part of execution.
